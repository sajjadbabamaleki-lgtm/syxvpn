What we do not claim

What we will not claim

Every VPN promises these. We will not, because nobody can deliver them.

We will not say "no logs at all"

Bytes are counted so your quota can be. Where you go is not recorded.

We will not say "always connected"

No software can create a route that does not exist. What we do is notice fast and move to the next one.

We will not say "fastest VPN in the world"

Speed depends on your distance to the gateway and the state of your network. We pick the best of what we have actually measured.

We claim no encryption of our own

We use standard, well-known protocols. Anyone claiming they built their own is someone to worry about.

FAQ

Questions

If the answer you want is not here, message support from inside the app.

How is SyxVPN different?

Two things. First, the gateway is chosen on measurement rather than guesswork — the app tests the whole path, not just whether the gateway answers. Second, configs you add yourself live in their own tab and are never touched.

Do you keep logs?

Bytes per subscription are counted, because that is what a quota is. Where you go is not recorded: gateway logging sits at warning level, so the addresses you open are never written down.

Is there an iPhone build?

Not yet — Android only for now. Until an iOS build actually ships, the App Store badge on this page stays disabled rather than sending you somewhere unrelated.

What happens when a gateway drops?

The app notices and reconnects; if that gateway will not answer, it moves to the next. When no healthy route is left it disconnects and says so, rather than pretending to be connected.

How does payment work?

In USDT on the TRON network, confirmed on chain. No subscription starts until the transfer has actually been seen.

Do I need an account?

No. The app opens without one. An account is only needed to buy a plan or move a subscription to another phone.
