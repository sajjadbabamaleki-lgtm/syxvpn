How it works

Three steps, and then you stop thinking about it.

Install

Take the Android file and install it. It opens straight onto the connect screen — no sign-up form, no email.

Switch on

Press the switch. The app measures the gateways, takes the best one and connects. Want a particular country? Pick it from the list.

Forget it

If the route drops, the app comes back. If a gateway fails, it moves to the next one.

What is actually in it

Not a feature list for a landing page — these are written, and they have tests.

Smart connect

The app does not just ask whether a gateway answers. It sends a real request through it. A gateway that replies in 20 ms but cannot reach the internet loses to one that takes 400 ms and works.

Purpose modes

Auto, Social, Streaming, Gaming. Each weighs latency, steadiness and a gateway's own record differently — they genuinely settle on different gateways, they are not four labels for one thing.

Configs stay yours

Configs you import live in their own tab. Nothing replaces, reorders or auto-selects them. Health is measured and shown; that is the entire extent of its authority.

Automatic recovery

When the tunnel drops, the app knows and comes back. Each failure is recorded against the gateway that caused it, so next time it reaches for one that has actually worked.

It fails closed

With no usable path left, the app disconnects and tells you. Showing "connected" over a tunnel that carries nothing is the worst thing a VPN can do.

Xray-core inside

The tunnel engine is Xray-core — what serious clients use — at a pinned version, not an unnamed build.

What we will not claim

Every VPN promises these. We do not, because nobody can keep them.

  • Not "no logs". Byte counters per subscription exist, because that is how a data quota works. Destinations do not: the gateways run at warning log level, so the addresses you open are not written down.
  • Not "always connected". No software can create a path that does not exist. What we can do is notice quickly that one has died and move to the next.
  • Not "the fastest VPN". Speed depends on your distance to the gateway and the state of your network. What we do is pick the best of what was actually measured.
  • No home-made cryptography. Standard, published protocols only. Anyone claiming their own encryption is a reason to worry, not to buy.

Questions

If yours is not here, message support from inside the app.

What makes cVPN different?

Two things. Selection is based on measurement rather than assumption: the app tests the whole path through a gateway, not merely whether the gateway answers. And the manual config section is genuinely separate — nothing ever reorders, replaces or re-picks a config you imported.

Do you keep logs?

Byte counters per subscription, because that is how a data quota works. Not destinations: the gateways run at warning log level, so the addresses you open are not written down.

Is there an iOS app?

Not yet. Android only. Until an iOS build actually ships, the App Store button on this page stays disabled rather than pointing somewhere irrelevant.

What happens when a server dies?

The app notices and reconnects; if that gateway will not answer it moves to the next. When no usable path is left it disconnects and says so.

How do payments work?

USDT on TRON, confirmed on chain. Nothing is unlocked until the transfer is actually seen.

Do I need an account?

No. The app opens straight onto the tunnel. An account is only needed to buy a plan or to carry a subscription to another phone.
